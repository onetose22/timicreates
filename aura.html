The problem

Many wallet apps bury simple actions under busy screens, confusing menus and tiny text. People just want to see their balance, send money and understand where it goes.

The solution

  • One clear balance card on the home screen
  • Four main actions within thumb reach
  • Simple weekly spending charts
  • A send flow in three taps or fewer

Design system

A calm indigo-to-cyan gradient, generous spacing, rounded cards and bold numbers.
